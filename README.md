## 👋 Hi, I’m Rini!
I am a clinician-scientist, and I use this space to share my research data and analysis code with the goal of making science transparent, accessible, and reproducible (see an [example](https://github.com/rinivarg/VRAdapt-Adults)). I also teach in the [ReproRehab](https://github.com/rinivarg/ReproRehab-Pod3) program, where I help rehabilitation researchers learn how to manage, analyze, and share their data and code.<br><br>
